Setting Up Your BigToe

Step 1: Connect

Connect to Big Toe on your network - it appears just like your home router.

Select Big Toe from the available network list to connect.

Step 2: Assign

Open a web browser and type in bigtoe.local

Using the Big Toe editor / monitor, add 'pedals' and assign MIDI events.

Step 3: Link Up

Connect MIDI on your phone, tablet, or computer to the Big Toe via Bluetooth.

Use your effects software to link your pedals to your effects.
